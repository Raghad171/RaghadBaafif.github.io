GRADUATION PROJECT • IN PROGRESS

SME Credit-Risk Prediction with Sector Indicators

An ongoing graduation project investigating whether supply-chain-related sector indicators provide additional predictive value for SME loan default prediction beyond loan, borrower, and macroeconomic information.

  • Research data: SBA loan records, macroeconomic variables, and manufacturing and wholesale sector indicators.
  • Planned comparison: loan-only models, models with macroeconomic controls, and models with additional sector indicators.
  • Planned prediction horizons: 24, 36, and 60 months, evaluated using time-aware validation.
  • Planned interpretation: examine feature importance and how indicator contributions vary across horizons.

Status: In Progress. Research and development are ongoing. Model evaluation and final results are pending.
