Data-Driven Early Detection of Rider Operational Issues

A data science research project focused on detecting operational issues affecting last-mile delivery riders earlier by integrating multiple operational data sources and identifying abnormal performance patterns.

  • Integrates operational indicators such as rider performance, targets, location-related information, and activity patterns
  • Explores anomaly detection and machine learning approaches for identifying early warning signals
  • Aims to distinguish between individual performance problems, rider-specific operational issues, and broader operational incidents
  • Designed to support supervisors with earlier and more data-driven intervention
